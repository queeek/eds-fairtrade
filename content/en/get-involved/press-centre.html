Press centre

If you are a journalist looking for information about Fairtrade and our latest press announcements, this section has you covered.

Are you working on a story for a global media outlet or international trade press and are seeking more information? Or would you like to interview a Fairtrade International representative?

Please contact Fairtrade International via press@fairtrade.net. Our communications team will be happy to assist you.

National or local media?

If your story is intended for national or local media and you are based in any of the highlighted countries on the map below, please contact your nearest Fairtrade organisation.
